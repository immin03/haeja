## Patterns that actually appear
1. **The viewer's situation is the hook, in the viewer's words.** "BETRAYED?" opens 3 of the 12 (#6, #8, #9). The others name the viewer's feeling or need: "let them go", "emotionally detach", "they broke you". None names the historical event.
2. **Second person, or "they" as the other person.** "YOU", "YOUR POWER", "THEY CAN'T FIND PEACE", "THOSE WHO HURT YOU". The viewer is the subject and the betrayer is "they".
3. **Question + command.** "BETRAYED? DO THIS", "BETRAYED? DON'T GET EVEN — GET AHEAD", "DON'T TAKE REVENGE / JUST DO THIS". These are a short question, then an instruction or promise.
4. **Payoff = power, peace or karma.** "take your power back", "watch karma work", "they can't find peace". Revenge is reframed as winning without revenge, which is exactly our story.
5. **Word count.** 4–9 words on the thumbnail, usually 2–4 lines, often one big line plus smaller lines. Our earlier 3-word cap was stricter than the market.
6. **Face.** Mostly a classical marble bust (8 of 12) or a realistic older Stoic face (2 of 12), placed on one side at 35–45% of the width. The expression is calm, pensive or stern, not shocked.
7. **Color.** Black or very dark background, white text plus ONE bright accent (yellow/gold in 10 of 12, sometimes red). There are no teal accents in the top results.
8. **Branding.** A small channel name or "MARCUS AURELIUS" line appears in a few (#1, #2). Lower-right is usually free.

## What this changes for us
- The hook text must state the viewer's situation ("BETRAYED?", "WHEN SOMEONE BETRAYS YOU") plus a promise ("DO THIS", "DON'T TAKE REVENGE"). Story phrases like "BURN THEIR NAMES" are out.
- Use yellow-gold as the accent and white as the main text color.
- Allow 4–7 words in two sizes.
- Keep a realistic, pensive or stern Marcus face (our differentiator). A marble-toned version is offered as one option, since busts dominate.
